<h1 align="center">IshVerse</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>Research, information and a clearer understanding.</strong></p>

IshVerse is building tools that help people work with information, inspect its sources and develop informed conclusions.

## What we are building

| Product | Purpose | Current stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IshVerse Intelligence** | A research workspace connecting company financials, documents, source evidence and collaborative research. | In development |
| **IshVerse Media** | A publication and editorial platform built around accountable sourcing and clear publishing workflows. | Product and architecture foundations |
| **IshVerse website** | Our company front door and product directory. | Website development |

## How we build

- **Evidence stays inspectable.** Sources, reporting dates, units and calculation inputs belong alongside conclusions.
- **Useful workflows come first.** We develop bounded research tasks and learn from actual use before expanding scope.
- **Access is deliberate.** Workspace permissions, private research and recovery receive attention throughout development.
- **Progress is described honestly.** A working prototype, a test result and a released product represent different milestones.
